## How to sign

Add a line to `CONTRIBUTORS.md` in the same pull request as your first
contribution:

```
Full Name <email@example.com> — signed Nundar ICLA, YYYY-MM-DD
```

Opening a pull request that adds this line is your electronic signature.

**Contributing on behalf of an employer?** Your employer must sign a Corporate
CLA before your contributions can be merged. Open an issue titled "CCLA request"
and we will send the document.
