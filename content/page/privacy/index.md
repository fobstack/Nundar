Data governance and intellectual property. Strict defense-grade confidentiality for proprietary customer schematics.

## 1. Automatic Mutual NDA for Submitted Drawings

All engineering drawings (STEP, IGES, DXF, PDF) sent to us with a custom quote request are treated as proprietary intellectual property. Files are stored on encrypted servers with restricted access solely for tooling feasibility analysis. We never distribute or share customer CAD designs with third parties.

## 2. Contact Information Usage

Contact emails and telephone numbers provided for RFQ requests are strictly utilized for technical specification clarification, quote generation and order fulfillment. We do not sell or rent marketing lists.

## 3. Export-Controlled Technical Data

Files marked as export controlled are partitioned into our secure aerospace server environment, with access restricted to authorized technical staff.
