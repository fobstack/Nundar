When a standard pack does not fit the joint, we make the fastener to your specification: non-standard head shapes, exact custom lengths, specific shoulder dimensions or specialized thread rolling. Parts are CNC precision turned or cold forged, from prototypes to volume production.

## What can be customized

### Head styles

- Socket head cap screw
- Button head screw
- Countersunk screw
- Hex head flange bolt
- Precision shoulder screw
- Bespoke turned part made to your drawing

The standard versions of these heads are in the catalog: [socket head cap screws](/collections/socket-head-cap-screws), [button head screws](/collections/button-head-screws), [countersunk screws](/collections/countersunk-screws), [hex head screws](/collections/hex-head-screws) and [shoulder screws](/collections/shoulder-screws).

### Thread sizes

- M3 × 0.5
- M4 × 0.7
- M5 × 0.8
- M6 × 1.0
- M8 × 1.25
- M10 × 1.5
- Any other dimension, described in your request

### Thread and shoulder lengths

- 10 mm
- 12 mm
- 16 mm
- 20 mm
- 25 mm
- 30 mm
- 40 mm
- 50 mm
- Any other specific length, described in your request

### Drive types

- Hex socket (standard)
- Torx drive (high torque)
- Phillips and slotted combination
- 12-point flange (aerospace grade)
- Spanner security socket
- No drive (smooth turned part)

### Venting

- Vented core hole for vacuum relief: a hollow center drilled through the head to relieve pressure in vacuum environments

### Materials

- Grade 5 titanium (Ti-6Al-4V), for ultimate strength
- Grade 2 titanium (commercially pure), for premium corrosion resistance
- Grade 7 palladium titanium alloy
- Bespoke aerospace special alloy

The [material guide](/material-guide) gives the composition and properties of each titanium grade, including Grade 23 (Ti-6Al-4V ELI). Name the grade you need in your request.

## Tolerances

- **CNC turned tolerance:** up to ±0.01 mm on dimensions.
- **Cold forging capability:** volume production of high-tensile socket screws and bolts.
- **Thread precision:** ISO Class 6g standard thread profile.

## How a request proceeds

1. Send the request with the form below. The form on this site takes text only.
2. An engineer reviews the request and replies by email within 2 to 4 business hours.
3. Send your drawing by reply email, as an engineering PDF, DXF, IGS or STEP file. Drawings always travel this way, after the first contact, because the form cannot take files.
4. Our senior engineering staff reviews the drawing and the specification, and we send formal pricing.
5. Both sides sign off the drawing. A signed-off custom drawing supersedes the standard catalog specifications.
6. A quotation is binding for 30 calendar days unless metal market billet surcharges fluctuate by more than 10%, and a purchase order becomes binding upon written confirmation from our engineering team. The [terms and conditions](/terms) give the details.

Every drawing we receive is treated as proprietary intellectual property under an automatic mutual NDA: see the [privacy policy](/privacy).

## What to send

In the request, describe the part in words:

- head style, drive type and whether a vented core hole is needed;
- nominal thread size and thread pitch;
- thread or shoulder length;
- material grade;
- required chamfer angles and particular head markings;
- the material certification level, such as EN 10204 3.1;
- any unique tolerances;
- the application: expected operating temperature, shear load parameters and target volume.

Then, by reply email, the engineering drawing or CAD file.
