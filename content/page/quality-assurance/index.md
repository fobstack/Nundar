Every titanium fastener is produced under AS9100D and ISO 9001:2015 certified controls, backed by EN 10204 3.1 chemical and mechanical inspection documentation. This page follows a fastener from the raw bar to the inspection certificate issued for its lot.

## From bar to finished fastener

### 1. Raw billet traceability

We source ASTM B348 / AMS 4928 Grade 5 bars exclusively from certified melting mills. Each lot is assigned a traceable heat code from ingot to finished fastener.

### 2. Class 6g thread gauging

Threads are rolled rather than cut, creating uninterrupted metallurgical grain flow. They are verified using calibrated optical comparators and hardened 6g go/no-go ring gauges.

### 3. Ultrasonic NDT scanning

For flight-critical applications, bars undergo ultrasonic immersion testing (AMS-STD-2154 Class A) to ensure zero internal voids, hydrogen inclusions or micro-cracks.

### 4. ASTM A967 passivation

Fasteners are ultrasonically degreased and nitric acid passivated to produce a chemically inert titanium dioxide (TiO2) protective skin that prevents galling and corrosion.

## Tests behind every certificate

### Chemical spectrometry

The chemical composition of the heat lot is measured by optical emission spectrometry (OES) calibrated against ASTM E2371 standards, and each element is compared with the AMS 4928 limits.

### Tensile testing

Tensile testing is conducted per ASTM E8 / ASTM F606 at 23°C (73.4°F) in an ambient cleanroom laboratory. Tensile strength, yield strength and elongation are each compared with the specification minimum.

### Dimensional inspection

Automated 3D laser CMM scanning samples 50 pieces per batch of 1,000. Every measured feature has to fall within the nominal drawing limits.

## What an inspection certificate contains

An inspection certificate identifies the order and the heat lot, then reports four groups of results: chemical spectrometry, tensile tests, the CMM dimensional matrix and regulatory statements. The table lists each field, taking a Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 socket screw as the example: the limits are those of that grade, and the dimensional rows are the features of that part.

| Field | What it states |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate type | EN 10204 Type 3.1 inspection certificate |
| Certificate number | The number that identifies this certificate |
| Order reference | The purchase order the parts were supplied against |
| Heat lot | The heat lot of the bar the parts were made from |
| Substrate | The alloy and grade, for example Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 |
| Chemical spectrometry: method | Optical emission spectrometry (OES) calibrated against ASTM E2371 standards |
| Chemical spectrometry: aluminum (Al) | The heat lot test result against the AMS 4928 range of 5.50% to 6.75%, and its conformance |
| Chemical spectrometry: vanadium (V) | The heat lot test result against the AMS 4928 range of 3.50% to 4.50%, and its conformance |
| Chemical spectrometry: iron (Fe) | The heat lot test result against the AMS 4928 maximum of 0.30%, and its conformance |
| Chemical spectrometry: oxygen (O) | The heat lot test result against the AMS 4928 maximum of 0.20%, and its conformance |
| Chemical spectrometry: titanium (Ti) | The balance of the composition, and its conformance |
| Tensile tests: method | ASTM E8 / ASTM F606 at 23°C (73.4°F) |
| Tensile tests: tensile strength (Rm) | The measured value in MPa against the specification minimum of 895 MPa |
| Tensile tests: yield strength (Rp0.2) | The measured value in MPa against the specification minimum of 828 MPa |
| Tensile tests: elongation in 4D (A) | The measured value in percent against the specification minimum of 10.0% |
| CMM dimensional matrix: sampling | Automated 3D laser CMM scanning of 50 pieces per batch of 1,000 |
| CMM dimensional matrix: overall length (L) | The nominal drawing target, the allowed tolerance, the measured CMM average and the status |
| CMM dimensional matrix: head diameter (dk) | The nominal drawing target, the allowed tolerance, the measured CMM average and the status |
| CMM dimensional matrix: socket across flats (s) | The nominal drawing target, the allowed tolerance, the measured CMM average and the status |
| Regulatory statements: RoHS 3 and REACH | Compliance with RoHS 3 (EU 2015/863) and REACH (EC 1907/2006): no lead, mercury, cadmium, hexavalent chromium, PBBs, PBDEs or SVHC candidate list substances above threshold levels |
| Regulatory statements: conflict minerals | Conformance with Dodd-Frank Section 1502: all titanium raw stock is sourced conflict-free, without supply chain involvement in the DRC or adjoining countries, with 3TG verification on file |

The thresholds behind the regulatory statements are set out in our [compliance declaration](/compliance).

## Documents supplied with a shipment

Standard pack shipments are accompanied by a certificate of conformance (CoC). Detailed EN 10204 3.1 chemical spectrometry and mechanical test reports are provided upon request: state the certification level you need when you ask for a quote.

## Verifying a heat lot

To verify the traceability of a batch, send us the laser-etched batch code or the heat lot number from your invoice through the [contact page](/contact). We answer from the mill certificate record of that batch: the bar stock specification, the heat analysis and the result of the 6g thread inspection.
