We combine metallurgical expertise with advanced multi-axis CNC lathe lines to engineer titanium fasteners, precision machined to rigid tolerances.

## The facility

Our plant is an AS9100D certified aerospace facility and is also certified to ISO 9001:2015. It runs multi-axis CNC lathe lines and a 5-axis synchronous thread rolling facility.

The facility is fully equipped with optical comparators, thread go/no-go plug instrumentation and ultrasonic flaw detection apparatus, to ensure zero-defect shipment of each titanium bolt, stud and nut specified. The [quality assurance](/quality-assurance) page describes the inspection process and the documents that come with a shipment.

## Three strengths

### Traceable raw billets

All materials map to verified ASTM heat lots with complete chemical testing certification sheets.

### ±0.01 mm tolerance

High-g military specification thread rolling with micro-honed shoulder grinding.

### Direct engineering

Our senior engineering staff reviews drawing schematics and CAD files within hours of RFQ submission. The [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing) page explains what can be made to order and how a request proceeds.
