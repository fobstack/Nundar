Need urgent prototype lead times, custom titanium alloys or large-volume production pricing? Our metallurgists and CNC engineers respond within 2 to 4 business hours. All inquiries are handled under strict mutual NDA compliance.

## Senior engineering

Direct engineering consultation. Our senior engineering staff reviews drawing schematics and CAD files and answers technical questions about a joint, an alloy or a tolerance.

## Technical RFQ submissions

Requests for quotation, for custom fasteners and for production contracts. Average quote generation: 2.4 hours.

## Urgent prototype turnaround

Dedicated rapid CNC lathe lines for AOG (Aircraft On Ground) support.

## Subjects we handle

- Custom titanium fastener RFQ, with drawing review
- High volume production contracts of 10,000 pieces or more
- Material traceability and EN 10204 3.1 certification requests
- Urgent prototype delivery in under 7 days
- Aerospace qualification and FAI testing consultation

## Manufacturing and testing plant

Our aerospace machining facility is certified to ISO 9001:2015 and AS9100D. The [about](/about) page presents the facility, and [quality assurance](/quality-assurance) describes our inspection process and certificates.

## What to include in your message

Describe the assembly and its operating environment:

- target thread pitches;
- expected operating temperature;
- shear load parameters;
- target volume;
- any unique aerospace specifications.

For a part made to your drawing, the [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing) page lists what can be specified. The form takes text only: drawings are sent by reply email after the first contact.
