Commercial master agreement. Last updated: October 2026.

## 1. Quotations and Purchase Orders

All Request for Quote (RFQ) estimates are binding for 30 calendar days from formal transmission, unless metal market billet surcharges fluctuate by more than 10%. Purchase Orders become binding upon written confirmation from our engineering team.

## 2. Tolerances and Inspection Conformance

Unless explicitly customized on customer drawings, standard fasteners adhere to ISO 2768-m dimensional tolerances and Class 6g metric pitch tolerances. Custom drawings supplied by the customer supersede standard catalog specifications upon mutual sign-off.

## 3. Material Certification (EN 10204 3.1)

Standard pack shipments are accompanied by a certificate of conformance (CoC). Detailed EN 10204 3.1 chemical spectrometry and mechanical test reports are provided upon request.

## 4. Shipping and Incoterms

Standard shipments are dispatched EXW / FCA or DAP per customer logistics accounts. AOG (Aircraft On Ground) priority rush handling is available with dedicated dispatch tracking.
