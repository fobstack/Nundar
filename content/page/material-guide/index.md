This guide gives the chemical composition, yield strengths, operating temperatures and standard specifications of the aerospace and industrial grades of titanium we machine into high-tolerance fasteners. Each grade has its own section, followed by a comparison of titanium with stainless steel and aerospace aluminum.

## Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V)

**Titanium alpha-beta high-tensile aerospace alloy.** Type: alpha-beta alloy. Specifications: UNS R56400, ASTM B348, AMS 4928.

The undisputed benchmark for high-stress mechanical fasteners where extreme strength-to-weight ratio and fatigue life are paramount.

### Composition of Grade 5

Chemical composition limits:

| Chemical element | Standard weight (%) |
| --- | --- |
| Titanium (Ti) | Balance (88-90%) |
| Aluminum (Al) | 5.5 – 6.75% |
| Vanadium (V) | 3.5 – 4.5% |
| Iron (Fe) | ≤ 0.40% |
| Oxygen (O) | ≤ 0.20% |
| Carbon (C) | ≤ 0.08% |

### Properties of Grade 5

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Density | 4.43 g/cm³ (44% lighter than steel) |
| Tensile strength (Rm) | 895 – 1,050 MPa |
| Yield strength (Rp0.2) | 828 – 920 MPa |
| Elongation | 10 – 14% |
| Hardness | 32 – 36 HRC (320 HV) |
| Maximum continuous temperature | 400°C (752°F) |
| Corrosion resistance | Excellent (marine, chloride, nitric acid) |

### Typical assemblies in Grade 5

- High-G aerospace structural fuselage bolts
- Formula 1 and supercar suspension upright pins
- Spacecraft payload clamp rings
- Marine racing hydrofoils and power transmissions

## Grade 2 (Commercially Pure)

**Unalloyed titanium workhorse for pure corrosion resistance.** Type: commercially pure alpha. Specifications: UNS R50400, ASTM B348, ASTM F67.

Ideal when corrosion immunity in oxidizing acids, wet chlorine or harsh marine brine takes priority over ultra-high tensile clamping strength.

### Composition of Grade 2

Chemical composition limits:

| Chemical element | Standard weight (%) |
| --- | --- |
| Titanium (Ti) | ≥ 99.2% |
| Iron (Fe) | ≤ 0.30% |
| Oxygen (O) | ≤ 0.25% |
| Carbon (C) | ≤ 0.08% |
| Nitrogen (N) | ≤ 0.03% |
| Hydrogen (H) | ≤ 0.015% |

### Properties of Grade 2

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Density | 4.51 g/cm³ |
| Tensile strength (Rm) | 345 – 485 MPa |
| Yield strength (Rp0.2) | 275 – 380 MPa |
| Elongation | 20 – 28% |
| Hardness | 80 – 90 HRB (160 HV) |
| Maximum continuous temperature | 315°C (600°F) |
| Corrosion resistance | Supreme (seawater, wet chlorine, bleach) |

### Typical assemblies in Grade 2

- Chemical plant heat exchanger fasteners
- Desalination plant piping flange screws
- Subsea sensor brackets and wet housings
- Chlorine and hypochlorite containment systems

## Grade 23 (Ti-6Al-4V ELI)

**Extra low interstitial medical and cryogenic titanium.** Type: alpha-beta, medical and cryogenic. Specifications: UNS R56401, ASTM F136, AMS 4930.

Selected when maximum fracture toughness at sub-zero cryogenic temperatures or strict surgical biocompatibility is mandatory.

### Composition of Grade 23

Chemical composition limits:

| Chemical element | Standard weight (%) |
| --- | --- |
| Titanium (Ti) | Balance |
| Aluminum (Al) | 5.5 – 6.5% |
| Vanadium (V) | 3.5 – 4.5% |
| Iron (Fe) | ≤ 0.25% |
| Oxygen (O) | ≤ 0.13% (strict) |
| Carbon (C) | ≤ 0.08% |

### Properties of Grade 23

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Density | 4.43 g/cm³ (44% lighter than steel) |
| Tensile strength (Rm) | 860 – 960 MPa |
| Yield strength (Rp0.2) | 790 – 860 MPa |
| Elongation | 12 – 18% |
| Hardness | 30 – 34 HRC (300 HV) |
| Maximum continuous temperature | 400°C; cryogenic service down to -250°C |
| Corrosion resistance | Superior biocompatibility and saline resistance |

### Typical assemblies in Grade 23

- Liquid nitrogen and liquid hydrogen rocket plumbing
- Medical surgical robotic arms and implants
- Ultra-low temperature cryogenic vacuum systems
- Non-magnetic 7T MRI operating equipment

## Grade 7 (Ti-0.15Pd)

**Palladium enhanced titanium for reducing acids.** Type: alpha with palladium, a noble metal addition. Specifications: UNS R52400, ASTM B348.

The gold standard for preventing crevice corrosion in low pH, high-temperature reducing acid environments where other metals dissolve.

### Composition of Grade 7

Chemical composition limits:

| Chemical element | Standard weight (%) |
| --- | --- |
| Titanium (Ti) | ≥ 99.0% |
| Palladium (Pd) | 0.12 – 0.25% |
| Iron (Fe) | ≤ 0.30% |
| Oxygen (O) | ≤ 0.25% |

### Properties of Grade 7

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Density | 4.51 g/cm³ |
| Tensile strength (Rm) | 345 – 485 MPa |
| Yield strength (Rp0.2) | 275 – 350 MPa |
| Elongation | 20 – 26% |
| Hardness | 85 HRB |
| Maximum continuous temperature | 350°C |
| Corrosion resistance | Ultimate (extreme crevice and reducing acids) |

### Typical assemblies in Grade 7

- Hydrochloric and sulfuric acid process pumps
- High-temperature chloride crevice joints
- Geothermal steam energy extraction wells
- Offshore oil and gas sour gas (H2S) platforms

## Titanium, stainless steel and aluminum compared

Why engineers move structural fasteners from stainless 316 and alloy steel to Grade 5 titanium:

| Performance metric | Titanium Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) | Stainless steel 316L | Aerospace aluminum 7075-T6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Density (mass) | 4.43 g/cm³ | 7.98 g/cm³ (+80% heavier) | 2.81 g/cm³ |
| Tensile strength | 895 – 1,050 MPa | 550 – 680 MPa | 570 MPa (poor fatigue) |
| Strength-to-weight ratio | 214 kN·m/kg | 75 kN·m/kg | 202 kN·m/kg |
| Marine / chloride immunity | 100% pitting immune | Prone to crevice corrosion | Corrodes in salt fog |
| Magnetic permeability | < 1.00005 (non-magnetic) | Slightly magnetic when worked | Non-magnetic |
