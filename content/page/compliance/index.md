Regulatory declaration. Effective date: October 2026. Certifying authority: our quality department.

## RoHS 3 (EU 2015/863)

We hereby certify that all titanium fasteners, precision shoulder bolts, nuts and bespoke CNC components manufactured under ASTM B348, AMS 4928 and ISO standard profiles comply strictly with the European Union Directive 2011/65/EU and amending Directive (EU) 2015/863 (RoHS 3).

Prohibited hazardous substances verified below thresholds:

| Substance | Verified level |
| --- | --- |
| Lead (Pb) | < 0.1% |
| Mercury (Hg) | < 0.1% |
| Cadmium (Cd) | < 0.01% |
| Hexavalent chromium (Cr VI) | < 0.1% |
| Polybrominated biphenyls (PBB) | < 0.1% |
| Phthalates (DEHP, BBP, DBP, DIBP) | < 0.1% |

## REACH (EC 1907/2006) candidate list of SVHCs

In accordance with Article 33 of Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006, our parts do not contain any of the Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC) on the current European Chemicals Agency (ECHA) candidate list in concentrations greater than 0.1% by weight.

## Conflict minerals (Section 1502)

Our raw titanium melting mills maintain full Conflict Minerals Reporting Templates (CMRT) verifying zero origin in the Democratic Republic of Congo or adjoining conflict areas.
