faq:
  - question: "What are the exact dimensional tolerances maintained by your CNC lathes?"
    answer: "Our standard machining processes maintain tolerances to ISO 2768-m (medium) class. For high-precision elements like ground shoulders and customized threads, we regularly hold tolerances up to ±0.01 mm on diameters and shoulder concentricity."
  - question: "What is the standard torque limit for an M5 Titanium Socket Head Cap Screw?"
    answer: "For a Grade 5 titanium M5 × 0.8 socket head cap screw preloaded to 70% of its 828 MPa yield strength, the tightening torque is approximately 4.5 N·m with molybdenum disulfide anti-seize paste (nut factor K = 0.11). Anti-seize lubricants reduce the friction coefficient, so the torque has to be adjusted to the lubricant to prevent over-tensioning: the same preload takes approximately 5.3 N·m with copper based anti-seize and 6.6 N·m with light machine oil. Dry threads would take approximately 9.0 N·m, but dry assembly of titanium threads leads to cold welding and thread galling and should be avoided. The fastener calculators page lists the torque for every thread size."
  - question: "What certifications are supplied with the custom fasteners?"
    answer: "Every batch can be supplied with an official EN 10204 3.1 Material Test Report (MTR). This certifies the chemical composition, heat treatment batch, tensile limits, and compliance with ASTM B348 / AMS 4928 parameters."

## Aerospace thread pitch standard

We machine threads to the tight Class 6g fitment profile, matching military standard MIL-S-8879 and aerospace UNJ thread profiles. This minimizes micro-slip vibration and structural fatigue in extreme atmospheric stress environments.

Torque figures for each thread size and lubricant are on the [fastener calculators](/tools/fastener-calculators) page, and the documents behind a certificate are described under [quality assurance](/quality-assurance).
