A fully threaded M3 × 0.5 button head screw in Grade 5 titanium (Ti-6Al-4V), 10 mm long, with a Torx drive and a natural burnished finish. The low, rounded head sits close to the surface, which suits covers, panels, instrument housings and compact mechanisms where a taller cap head would be in the way. In Grade 5 titanium it is also non-magnetic, so it can be used close to imaging and measuring equipment.

## Why this fastener

### Low-profile head

The button head keeps the fastener low and smooth above the joint. On bodywork the same shape gives a low-drag profile.

### Torx drive

The Torx recess is our high-torque drive option, a good match for a thread this small.

### Non-magnetic

Grade 5 titanium has a relative magnetic permeability below 1.00005. 316L stainless steel, by comparison, becomes slightly magnetic when it is worked.

### Light and strong

The alloy has a density of 4.43 g/cm³ and a tensile strength of 895 – 1,050 MPa. Replacing a steel fastener saves about 44% of its weight. The [material guide](/material-guide) has the full data.

Next to carbon fiber composite, titanium avoids the galvanic corrosion that attacks steel and aluminum. Larger button heads for [motorsport](/industries/motorsport) bodywork, such as an M5 × 16 mm size, are made to order through [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing).

### Hull fairings

The same made-to-order M5 × 16 mm button head is specified as a hull fairing screw in [marine and offshore energy](/industries/marine-offshore-energy), where titanium does not suffer crevice pitting.

These screws are forged and turned exclusively from fully traceable, heat-treated Grade 5 Ti-6Al-4V billets. On request we supply:
