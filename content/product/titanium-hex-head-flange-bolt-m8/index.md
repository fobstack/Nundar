A partially threaded M8 × 1.25 hex head flange bolt in Grade 5 titanium (Ti-6Al-4V), 30 mm long, with a natural finish. The external hex is driven with a wrench or socket, and the flange under the head acts as an integrated load-spreading washer face. It is a structural bolt for chassis members, housings and mounts, and the largest thread in our standard catalog.

## Why this fastener

### Integrated washer face

The flange spreads the clamp load over the joint surface, so there is no separate washer to fit or lose.

### Plain shank under the head

The bolt is partially threaded, which leaves an unthreaded shank between the flange and the thread.

### Grade 5 strength

Tensile strength is 895 – 1,050 MPa and yield strength 828 – 920 MPa, at a density of 4.43 g/cm³. Replacing a steel bolt saves about 44% of its weight while maintaining or exceeding shear tolerances. The [material guide](/material-guide) has the full data.

### Assemble with anti-seize

Titanium threads assembled dry are at high risk of cold welding and galling under preload, so we recommend an anti-seize paste such as molybdenum disulfide. Our [fastener calculators](/tools/fastener-calculators) work out the tightening torque and preload for an M8 × 1.25 bolt in Grade 5.
