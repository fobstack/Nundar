A fully threaded M4 × 0.7 button head screw in Grade 5 titanium (Ti-6Al-4V), 12 mm long, with a hex socket drive and a blue anodized finish. The low, rounded head suits covers, panels and enclosures where a taller cap head would be in the way, and the anodized surface sets it apart from the natural-finish screws in our catalog.

## Why this fastener

### Anodized blue finish

When titanium is paired directly with aluminum in a maritime environment, it can act as a cathode. For those joints we recommend anodization or isolation washers. This screw is supplied anodized.

### Low-profile head

The button head keeps the fastener low and smooth above the joint, with no corners to catch.

### Standard hex socket

The hex socket is our standard drive, so the screw is installed with an ordinary hex key.

### Grade 5 strength

Grade 5 has a yield strength of 828 – 920 MPa, more than double that of commercially pure Grade 2, at a density of 4.43 g/cm³. The [material guide](/material-guide) compares the grades.

## Also specified for

### Surgical robotics and MRI suites

In our [7 Tesla MRI case study](/case-studies/mri-compatible-surgical-robotics), Grade 5 M3 and M4 button head screws reached a relative magnetic permeability below 1.00005 and left zero artifacts on clinical scans. Those parts were made with a Torx drive, an electropolished surface and cleanroom packaging. Variations like these are handled through [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing).

### Carbon fiber assemblies

Next to carbon fiber composite, titanium avoids the galvanic corrosion that attacks steel and aluminum, which is why button heads are specified for [motorsport](/industries/motorsport) bodywork.

### Marine hardware

Grade 5 titanium is rated excellent against marine and chloride corrosion. Typical uses are described under [marine and offshore energy](/industries/marine-offshore-energy).

## Traceability and certification

These screws are forged and turned exclusively from fully traceable, heat-treated Grade 5 Ti-6Al-4V billets. On request we supply:
