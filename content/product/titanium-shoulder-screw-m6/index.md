A shoulder screw with an M6 × 1.0 thread and a precision ground Ø8 mm shoulder, 20 mm long and partially threaded. The unthreaded shoulder is the working surface: it locates, guides or carries a moving part, while the thread only holds the screw in place. Typical uses are pivots, axles and locating pins in linkages, suspension uprights and articulating joints. This catalog item is listed in a custom alloy, which you specify with your inquiry.

## Why this fastener

### Precision ground shoulder

We hold tolerances up to ±0.01 mm on precision ground shoulder shanks, on both diameter and shoulder concentricity.

### Rolled class 6g thread

Threads are rolled rather than cut, which leaves the grain flow of the metal uninterrupted, and they are verified with hardened 6g go/no-go ring gauges.

### Alloy matched to the load

Our engineering metallurgists review proprietary alloy requirements under mutual NDA. The [material guide](/material-guide) sets out the properties of the standard titanium grades as a starting point.

### Shoulder dimensions to drawing

A different shoulder diameter or length, or an undercut shoulder shank, is made to order through [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing).

## Also specified for

### Motorsport suspension uprights

In our [suspension upright case study](/case-studies/suspension-upright-shoulder-bolts), custom Grade 5 titanium shoulder bolts with a Ø8 mm ground shank held to +0.000/-0.008 mm replaced steel hex bolts that had to be changed every 1,200 km. They were tested to a service life of more than 6,500 km. More under [motorsport](/industries/motorsport).

Our titanium fasteners are forged and turned exclusively from fully traceable, heat-treated billets. On request we supply:

- a chemical analysis certificate to EN 10204 3.1
- a dimensional and class 6g thread conformity report
- ultrasonic non-destructive testing (NDT) reports
