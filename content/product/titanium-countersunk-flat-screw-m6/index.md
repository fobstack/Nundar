A fully threaded M6 × 1.0 flat countersunk screw in commercially pure Grade 2 titanium, 16 mm long, with a natural finish. The conical head seats in a countersunk hole and finishes flush with the surface, so nothing stands proud of a lid, cover, fairing or guide rail. In Grade 2 it is the screw to choose when corrosion resistance matters more than clamping strength.

## Why this fastener

### Flush 90° head

The flat countersunk 90° head leaves the joint surface level, with no head to snag, seal around or disturb the flow over it.

### Grade 2 for corrosion resistance

Unalloyed Grade 2 is ideal when corrosion immunity in oxidizing acids, wet chlorine or harsh marine brine takes priority over ultra-high tensile clamping strength. It resists seawater, wet chlorine and bleach.

### A self-healing oxide film

Austenitic and duplex stainless steels suffer chloride pitting and crevice corrosion in warm seawater, often beneath the screw head. Titanium develops a self-healing TiO2 passive oxide film instead.

### Strength to plan around

Tensile strength is 345 – 485 MPa and yield strength 275 – 380 MPa. Grade 5 has more than double the yield strength, and where the joint needs it we make countersunk screws in Grade 5 to order through [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing). The [material guide](/material-guide) compares the two grades.

## Also specified for

### Subsea pressure enclosures

In our [subsea case study](/case-studies/subsea-pressure-enclosures), passivated Grade 2 and Grade 5 titanium countersunk screws replaced 316L stainless fasteners that had pitted, galled and seized. The enclosures were certified to 300 bar and deployed for 24 months without seizing.

### Chemical and desalination plant

Grade 2 fasteners are used in chemical plant heat exchangers, desalination plant piping flanges, and chlorine and hypochlorite containment systems. See [marine and offshore energy](/industries/marine-offshore-energy).

### Surgical and vacuum fixtures

Smaller flat countersunk screws, such as an M3 × 8 mm size, are specified as flush fasteners for surgical guide rails and vacuum chamber fixtures. These are made to order through [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing).
