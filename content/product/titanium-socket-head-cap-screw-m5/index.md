A fully threaded M5 × 0.8 socket head cap screw in Grade 5 titanium (Ti-6Al-4V), with a cylindrical head, a hex socket drive and a natural burnished finish. It replaces a steel or stainless M5 cap screw wherever weight matters and strength cannot be given up: brackets, trusses, housings and other high-performance assemblies. We supply it in 10, 16, 20 and 25 mm lengths, and M5 × 0.8 is one of the thread sizes we are asked for most.

## Why this fastener

### A 44% lighter joint

Grade 5 titanium has a density of 4.43 g/cm³, against 7.93 g/cm³ for stainless steel. Replacing a steel fastener with this one cuts its weight by about 44% while maintaining or exceeding shear tolerances. The [material guide](/material-guide) gives the full mechanical and chemical data for the alloy.

### Rolled class 6g threads

Threads are rolled rather than cut, which leaves the grain flow of the metal uninterrupted, and they are verified with hardened 6g go/no-go ring gauges.

### Torque worked out, not guessed

Titanium threads assembled dry are at high risk of galling, so an anti-seize lubricant is the condition we recommend. The right tightening torque depends on the preload the joint needs and on that lubricant; the [fastener calculators](/tools/fastener-calculators) set out the formula and the figures for each thread size.

### Modified to order

A longer screw, a partial thread, a fine pitch, a vented core for vacuum relief or a safety wire hole through the head are all made to order through [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing).

## Also specified for

### Aerospace structures

The 20 mm length is specified in [aerospace and defense](/industries/aerospace-defense) as a high-tensile truss bolt. In our [stage separation case study](/case-studies/orbital-stage-separation-fasteners), M5 × 16 mm screws with cold-rolled threads and cross-drilled safety wire holes cut joint weight by 44% and qualified to a 28g RMS vibration profile.

### Cleanroom and vacuum tools

For [semiconductor and vacuum](/industries/semiconductor-vacuum) equipment the 20 mm length is supplied degreased and double bagged for ISO 5 cleanrooms.

### Surgical robotics

A passivated, cleanroom-packaged version that withstands autoclaving is used in [medical and surgical robotics](/industries/medical-surgical-robotics).

## Traceability and certification

These screws are forged and turned exclusively from fully traceable, heat-treated Grade 5 Ti-6Al-4V billets. On request we supply:

- a chemical analysis certificate to EN 10204 3.1
- a dimensional and class 6g thread conformity report
- ultrasonic non-destructive testing (NDT) reports
