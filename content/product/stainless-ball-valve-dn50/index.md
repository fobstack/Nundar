A full-bore two-piece ball valve machined from 316L stainless steel. Rated to
1000 PSI at ambient temperature with a PTFE seat, it handles corrosive and
food-grade media without contamination. Supplied with ISO 5211 mounting for
direct actuator fitting.

## Why this valve

### 316L stainless body

Resists chloride pitting in seawater and chemical service where 304 fails.

### Full bore, zero flow restriction

The bore matches the pipe ID, so pressure drop across the valve is negligible.

## Also specified for

### Food-grade dosing and CIP circuits

The PTFE seat carries no plasticiser migration risk, and the full bore leaves
no dead volume where product can stagnate between CIP cycles.
