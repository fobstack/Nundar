## The demand

In Formula 1, Le Mans prototypes and premier GT racing, every gram of unsprung rotational mass directly degrades damper compliance, cornering grip and tire life. The fasteners that carry a wheel upright, a brake caliper or a heat shield are part of that mass, and they also have to survive:

- **Curb strikes** that deliver instant 5g+ multi-directional shear spikes.
- **Heat** from exhaust manifolds and brake rotors close by, with localized temperatures exceeding 450°C.
- **The calendar.** Parts are needed between race weekends, on lead times under 5 business days.

## Why titanium here

Titanium takes weight out of the unsprung assembly while keeping the joint reliable in shear. We manufacture centerless ground titanium shoulder pivots, wheel upright bolts and turbo heat-shield fasteners engineered for sustained 950+ MPa cyclic shear.

The alloys we recommend are Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) and Grade 5 heat-treated to Condition STA. Grade 5 also suits composite structures: paired with carbon fiber, titanium avoids the galvanic oxidation that makes steel or aluminum corrode rapidly beside it, which matters for bodywork fasteners.

For a worked example, see our case study on [suspension upright shoulder bolts](/case-studies/suspension-upright-shoulder-bolts).
