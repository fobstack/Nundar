## The demand

Trapped virtual leaks beneath blind fastener holes can delay semiconductor chamber pump-down cycles by days. Inside a chamber held at 10⁻¹⁰ Torr, a fastener is judged by what it releases:

- **Trapped air.** Micro-volumes of air caught under a screw cause catastrophic virtual vacuum leaks.
- **Contamination.** There is zero tolerance for particle shedding or hydrocarbon molecular contamination.
- **Bake-out.** Ultra-high vacuum (UHV) chamber conditioning bakes the hardware at temperatures up to 250°C.

## Why titanium here

We engineer precision center-bored vented titanium fasteners that let trapped air exhaust smoothly without compromising bolt tensile strength. The vent is a hollow center drilled through the head, which relieves the pocket of gas at the bottom of a blind hole. Fasteners are delivered 100% degreased for extreme EUV wafer fabrication tools.

We recommend Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) and ultra-clean Grade 2 commercially pure titanium. Both are rated above the bake temperature: the maximum continuous temperature is 400°C for Grade 5 and 315°C for Grade 2. Full data for each grade is in the [material guide](/material-guide).
