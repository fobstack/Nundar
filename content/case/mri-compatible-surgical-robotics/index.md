## The challenge

An advanced medical diagnostics and robotic surgery group builds robotic end-effectors that work inside a 7.0 Tesla MRI scanner, for precision intraoperative navigation. At that field strength, any magnetic trace in a surgical robot joint creates distorting image shadows on the scan. Those shadows make sub-millimeter navigation of brain tissue inaccurate and potentially dangerous.

The joints needed button head screws with zero magnetic interference.

## What we made

We produced cleanroom-packaged Grade 5 titanium button head screws in M3 and M4. Trace elements were strictly controlled, and the screws achieved a relative magnetic permeability below 1.00005.

Titanium is the natural choice for this joint because it is non-magnetic, where stainless steel 316L turns slightly magnetic when it is worked. The extra-low interstitial form of the alloy is the one selected for surgical robotic arms and for equipment that operates around a 7T magnet.

The specification, as verified:

- Grade 5 ELI (extra-low interstitial) titanium
- Torx T10 and T15 drive heads
- Electropolished smooth surface
- Cleanroom ultrasonic degreasing

The M3 screw has the head and drive of the [M3 × 0.5 titanium button head Torx screw](/products/titanium-button-head-torx-screw-m3) in our catalog. The catalog M3 screw has a natural burnished finish and the catalog M4 button head has a hex drive, so the electropolished finish and the M4 Torx screw were made to order through [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing).

## The outcome

- **Relative magnetic permeability (µr) below 1.00005.**
- **0 artifacts on 7T clinical MRI scans.** The screws leave no shadow for the navigation system to work around.
- **Class 10,000 cleanroom packaging**, after ultrasonic degreasing.
- **Traceability ready for ISO 13485.**
