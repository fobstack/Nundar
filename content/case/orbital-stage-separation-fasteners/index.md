## The challenge

A commercial space launch operator, preparing a Class A mission, needed to re-engineer the payload bracket bolts in its stage separation and avionics bay structure. The bolts had to withstand 28g RMS random vibration while shedding crucial vehicle mass.

The existing hardware did neither well. During qualification shaking, standard high-tensile steel fasteners added 3.2 kg of unnecessary parasitic mass. They also suffered micro-thread fretting under the severe acoustic vibration profiles of atmospheric ascent.

## What we made

We supplied precision-forged Grade 5 Ti-6Al-4V socket head cap screws in two sizes, M5 × 16 mm and M6 × 20 mm. Both have cold-rolled UNJ threads to MIL-S-8879 and dual cross-drilled safety wire lock holes through the cylindrical head.

The thread form is the answer to the fretting. Our threads are rolled rather than cut, which leaves the grain flow of the metal uninterrupted, and the Class 6g fit with a UNJ profile minimizes micro-slip vibration and structural fatigue. The safety wire holes let each screw be locked against rotation once it is torqued.

The specification, as verified:

- Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 to AMS 4928
- Class 6g thread with a UNJ controlled root radius
- Ultrasonic non-destructive inspection
- Safety wire holes pre-drilled in the cap

M5 × 16 mm is a catalog size of our [M5 × 0.8 titanium socket head cap screw](/products/titanium-socket-head-cap-screw-m5) (TI-SHC-M5-16). The M6 × 20 mm screw and the safety wire drilling are not catalog items; we produce them through [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing).

## The outcome

- **Joint weight fell by 44%** against the steel fasteners.
- **The joints qualified to the 28g RMS vibration profile.**
- **Zero fatigue failures in 14 hot-fires.**
- **100% lot traceability to ASTM B348.**
