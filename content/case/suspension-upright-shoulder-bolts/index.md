## The challenge

A European endurance prototype racing team was losing the bolts in its suspension uprights to fatigue. The standard steel hex bolts suffered continuous shear fatigue under repeated 4.5g lateral curb impacts at high speed. They had to be replaced every 1,200 km, and a failure risked catastrophic dislocation of the knuckle joint.

The aim was to eliminate play in the upright and shear failure over the curbs of a 24-hour endurance race, using precision-ground titanium pivots.

## What we made

We custom engineered and manufactured Grade 5 titanium precision shoulder bolts with:

- an Ø8 mm ground shank held to a tolerance of +0.000/-0.008 mm
- CNC rolled threads
- an optimized 0.8 mm underhead transition radius

The specification, as verified: a centerless ground Ø8.0 mm shank, Grade 5 heat-treated titanium, a burnished natural finish and a modified low-profile head.

The ground shank is what removes the play: the bolt works as the pivot of the upright, so its shoulder has to fit the bore closely and run true to the thread. Titanium also takes mass out of an unsprung assembly, where every gram degrades damper compliance, cornering grip and tire life.

The Ø8 mm shoulder is the diameter of the [M6 titanium shoulder screw](/products/titanium-shoulder-screw-m6) in our catalog. The low-profile head and the underhead radius were engineered for this upright; work of that kind goes through [custom manufacturing](/custom-manufacturing).

## The outcome

- **Service life tested to 6,500+ km**, against replacement every 1,200 km for the steel bolts.
- **Shoulder concentricity of ±0.005 mm.**
- **A 9-day turnaround to track testing.**
- **Tensile strength tested at 950 MPa.**
