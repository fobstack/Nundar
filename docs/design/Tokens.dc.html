Geometry

Radius
2px on inputs and buttons, 0 on panels. Sharp corners read as precision; rounded ones read as consumer software.
Spacing
4px base. Steps 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96. Section gaps use 64 and above.
Measure
Body copy capped at 68ch, lead paragraphs at 60ch. Long specification pages stay readable.
Elevation
Borders, not shadows. A 1px line separates; a shadow implies a floating layer that a catalogue does not have.
